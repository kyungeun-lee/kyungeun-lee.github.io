Research

Our research focuses on understanding and improving the reliability of deep learning systems, with an emphasis on information-theoretic analysis, tabular data, and real-world decision problems.

Key Problems We Tackle

  • Reliability and failure modes of deep learning systems
  • Representation collapse and spurious correlations
  • Anomaly and out-of-distribution detection in tabular data

Our Approach

  • Information-theoretic analysis of learned representations
  • Lightweight and deployable learning algorithms
  • Diagnostics and monitoring for real-world ML systems

Application Domains

  • Finance, healthcare, and industrial tabular data
  • Anomaly detection and system monitoring
  • Benchmarking and evaluation of learning algorithms
